# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People experiencing late-night thoughts, loneliness, racing minds, or quiet moments who need a present, judgment-free voice companion to talk through their feelings, reflect, and decompress.

## Product Purpose

Whispr provides an empathetic, real-time AI voice companion for late-night reflection and quiet moments, offering warm, natural conversation without judgment or unsolicited problem-solving.

## Positioning

An empathetic digital sanctuary built to listen and comfort—not a productivity assistant or task solver. Differentiated by local-first processing, ephemeral memory with single-tap deletion, and distinct conversational personas engineered for warmth and emotional resonance.

## Operating Context

Late-night and solitary environments (bedside, dark rooms, quiet moments), where low-light ambiance, subdued audio feedback, gentle typography, and airtight privacy assurances are critical.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Web application stack: React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite.
- Backend / data: Supabase (`waitlist_signups` table for early access).
- Core personas: The Calm Listener (gentle, present), The Energetic Friend (upbeat, witty), The Motivational Coach (direct, inspiring).
- Audio and speech: Hyper-realistic vocal models, interactive waveform visualization, local-first audio processing, ephemeral memory.
- Scope: Maintaining the marketing landing page while shaping the full interactive voice web application.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Whispr (Whispr AI).
- Voice & Tone: Empathetic, warm, intimate, calm, judgment-free, respectful of quiet hours.
- Aesthetic baseline: Dark, ambient nighttime world with deep navy surfaces, soft organic glows, and warm amber accents.

- Working landing page codebase in `src/` ([App.tsx](file:///media/redratmoon/New%20Volume1/stuff/projectW-main/src/App.tsx)) with interactive waveform, persona cards, and Supabase waitlist integration.

## Product Principles

1. **Empathy over Utility:** Built to listen and reflect, not to optimize tasks or deliver unsolicited solutions.
2. **Sanctuary-Grade Privacy:** Local-first voice processing and ephemeral memory by default; what is shared in the dark stays in the dark.
3. **Calm by Design:** Intentionally crafted for nighttime use with subdued contrasts, gentle motion, and zero aggressive interruption.
4. **Persona Distinction:** Each companion persona possesses a clearly articulated, distinct emotional tone and interaction style.
